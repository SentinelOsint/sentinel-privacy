Sentinel – Field Intelligence: Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE OF PUBLICATION]

Sentinel – Field Intelligence ("Sentinel", "the App", "we") is an iPhone and iPad app for investigators, security professionals and other professionals doing lawful research. This policy explains what data the App handles, where it goes and what we keep.

1. Data stored on your device

Your cases, field notes, search history, saved briefs, audit log and settings are stored on your device in encrypted storage (AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption). The encryption keys are kept in the iOS Keychain and do not leave your device. We do not have access to this data, and there is no cloud sync.

Password breach checks are never saved: the password you check is not written to your search history, audit log, cases or reports.

The App also keeps a few items in the Keychain so that they survive reinstalling the App: a random install identifier, the trial start date and usage counters for trial and plan limits.

2. Data sent to our server

Some features need our server (hosted on Railway in the United States). Depending on what you use, the App sends:

  • Wanted, sanctions and screening checks: the name you search for, checked against our copies of the OFAC, UN, EU, BIS and Canadian sanctions lists, the U.S. Consolidated Screening List, the UK Sanctions List and the HHS OIG List of Excluded Individuals/Entities, and forwarded to Interpol's public Red Notices service.
  • AI features (Field Brief, Full Analysis, Web Brief, Case Intake, case reports, field note and timeline analysis, image forensics interpretation): the information you submit for that analysis – such as names, identifiers, findings and notes, depending on the feature, the search term and results, and the notes, case details or intake text you entered. If you link a search to a case, the Field Brief and Full Analysis include that case's field notes.
  • Image Forensics: the image you choose is sent to our server for metadata, error-level, compression, hidden-data and perceptual-hash analysis. The server processes it in memory and does not store it. If you then request an AI interpretation, only the text results of those checks, including the image's metadata, are sent on to Anthropic – not the image itself.
  • Other lookups: domains, vehicle VINs, password breach checks (only the first 5 characters of a password hash) and IP threat checks (and, in older versions of the App, court searches) are passed through our server to the services listed in section 4.
  • App integrity: Apple App Attest and DeviceCheck data generated by your device, so our server can confirm requests come from a genuine copy of the App. This data does not contain your searches or case content.
  • Subscriptions: Apple's signed transaction data, so we can confirm your subscription.

Sentinel does not keep your AI requests or AI responses on its server, and its application code does not write your search terms, notes, prompts or AI responses to its logs. Interpol Red Notice results may be cached in the server's memory for up to 1 hour, keyed by a one-way hash of the name searched, and are cleared when the server restarts. Our hosting provider's infrastructure may record request URLs in its logs; for some wanted, sanctions and other lookups these URLs can contain the name or identifier you searched for.

2a. AI processing and your permission

AI features are processed by Anthropic, PBC (United States), which provides the Claude AI models. Information is sent for AI processing only when you start an AI feature, and only after you have allowed AI data sharing. The App asks for your permission the first time you use an AI feature. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy; the App then asks again before the next AI request. Declining sends nothing and uses none of your plan's allowance.

The FBI wanted search is sent directly from the App to api.fbi.gov. Interpol, sanctions and screening checks go through our server.

2b. Voice dictation, microphone and photos

Voice dictation (for example in Case Intake and Field Notes) uses Apple's Speech framework. The App uses the microphone only while you are dictating, after you have granted microphone and speech recognition permission. Your speech may be processed by Apple to convert it to text, under Apple's privacy policy. Sentinel does not receive or store the audio; the resulting text stays on your device unless you send it for AI processing as described above.

The App accesses only the photos you choose, for Image Forensics and reverse image search.

3. What our server keeps

To enforce trial and plan limits and protect the service, our server stores:

  • your random install identifier with your lifetime trial usage counts and the date it was last used,
  • for subscribers, Apple's original transaction ID with your plan level, monthly usage counts and subscription dates,
  • App Attest key data linked to your install identifier,
  • a daily total of AI costs.

These records contain no names, search terms or case content. They are deleted 365 days after they were last used. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical request logs, which can include your IP address, the App version and device type, the time of the request and the request URLs described above.

4. Third-party services

To provide its features, the App or our server sends the data needed for each request to these services:

  • Anthropic (Claude): processes AI requests from our server. Web Brief also uses Anthropic's web search.
  • Interpol public Red Notices service: names you search.
  • FBI public Wanted API: the last name you search, sent directly from the App.
  • SEC EDGAR: company names. Markbase: company names on the Company screen.
  • IPinfo: IP addresses you look up or monitor, including the IP addresses of domains you look up, for country and network information. Google Public DNS and crt.sh: domains you look up. Older versions of the App also use ipapi.co and ip-api.com for IP lookups.
  • NHTSA: vehicle VINs, and the make, model and year of the vehicle to check recalls. CourtListener: court search terms in older versions of the App. The current version opens CourtListener in your browser instead.
  • Have I Been Pwned: only the first 5 characters of a password hash, never the password itself.
  • Open-Meteo: coordinates on the Geo screen. MapLibre: map tiles for the area you view.
  • Your own API keys: if you add your own keys for Shodan, AbuseIPDB or GreyNoise, the related searches are sent to those services under your account. For AbuseIPDB and GreyNoise, the lookup and your API key are relayed through Sentinel's server to reach the service; they are not stored or logged by Sentinel's server application. The IP address is part of the request URL, which our hosting provider's logs may record (see section 3); your API key is sent in a header, not in the URL.
  • Apple: handles all payments, App Attest and DeviceCheck, and speech recognition for dictation. We never receive your payment details.
  • Older versions of the App also sent data to OpenCorporates (company names), Reddit (usernames) and Tracerfy (searches made with your own key). The current version only links to OpenCorporates and Reddit and no longer uses Tracerfy.

Each service handles the data under its own privacy policy. Links to other websites that you open in your browser are governed by those websites.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it for advertising.
  • The App contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • Sentinel does not use your data to train AI models.

6. Professional and lawful use

Sentinel is for lawful professional research. It is not a consumer reporting agency and must not be used for employment, tenant, credit, insurance or other eligibility decisions. You are responsible for complying with applicable laws, including the FCRA, DPPA and state and provincial privacy laws.

Sentinel is not intended for anyone under 18.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can delete all data on your device at any time in Settings → Security → Wipe All Data, or by deleting the App. The Keychain items in section 1 may remain on the device after deleting the App.
  • You can choose not to use AI features, dictation or the lookups that send data to third parties, and you can withdraw your AI data-sharing permission at any time in Settings → Privacy. You can turn off microphone, speech recognition and photo access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Depending on where you live, including California and Canada, you may have the right to access or delete personal information we hold about you. Contact us and we will respond within the time required by law.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
